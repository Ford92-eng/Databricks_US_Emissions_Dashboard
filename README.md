# Databricks_US_Emissions_Dashboard
Databricks dashboard analysing US emissions data and key trends.

## Project Overview
Created an interactive dashboard in Databricks to analyse US emissions data and identify emissions trends across states and counties.

## Tools Used
- Databricks
- Genie
- SQL

## Analysis
- Analysed US emissions per person.
- Identified the top 10 US states by total emissions.
- Calculated the percentage of national emissions contributed by the top 10 US states.
- Identified the top 10 US counties by total emissions.

## Key Insights
- The top 10 states account for approximately 51% of total US emissions.
- Maricopa County, Arizona and Harris County, Texas were among the counties with the highest total emissions.
- Emissions were compared with population to explore differences across the counties.
